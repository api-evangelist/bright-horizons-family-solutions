# Bright Horizons Family Solutions

Bright Horizons Family Solutions Inc. (NYSE: **BFAM**) is the largest provider of employer-sponsored child care in the United States. Founded in 1986 by Linda Mason and Roger Brown and headquartered in Newton, Massachusetts, the company operates more than **1,000 early-education and child-care centers** across the US, UK, Netherlands, and India, serving roughly **1,450 employer clients** and **30,000+ employees worldwide**. Bright Horizons sells exclusively into enterprise HR and benefits buyers — there is no public API, SDK, developer portal, status page, or active GitHub organization.

## Business Segments

### 1. Full Service Center-Based Child Care
On-site, near-site, and consortium child-care centers operated for enterprise employers. Programs cover infants, toddlers, preschool, and kindergarten prep, with international operations including Teddies Nurseries (UK, acquired 2009), Kidsunlimited (UK, 2013), Asquith Court (UK, 2016), and Kindergarten (Netherlands).

### 2. Back-Up Care
On-demand backup care for **children, adults/elders, and pets**, delivered through a network of Bright Horizons centers, partner centers, and in-home caregivers. Sold as an employer-paid benefit so that employees can stay at work when their primary care arrangement breaks down.

### 3. Educational Advisory Services
Workforce education and family advisory services for employers:

- **EdAssist by Bright Horizons** — Tuition assistance, student loan repayment, and workforce education program administration. **257 employer clients**, **4.7 million eligible learners**, **$1B+ in education and loan payments administered annually**, and **352,000+ active program participants**. First provider to address student loan debt as an employee benefit.
- **College Coach** — College admissions and financial-aid coaching delivered as an employee benefit (acquired 2006).
- **Workforce Consulting** — Strategy and program design for work/life and family benefits.
- **Care Advising** — Launched May 2026 to support families navigating complex caregiving needs.

### 4. Sittercity
Consumer caregiver marketplace (acquired 2020) connecting families with babysitters, nannies, pet sitters, senior companions, and tutors. **140,000+ five-star reviews**, available in **96% of US neighborhoods**. Offered to enterprise users as a Bright Horizons benefit.

## Developer Surface
